## Job fw-F6, 29 Oct 2026

Text: Livy, *Ab urbe condita* 3.26–29 (B. O. Foster's Loeb and Canon Roberts's 1912 translations; Roberts's read on Perseids, https://fgh.perseids.org/read/latinLit/phi0914/phi001/perseus-eng3/3.26.3-3.26.11 and .../3.26.5-3.27.7). 3.13 for Caeso's bail.

Doubts noted for the historian check: Livy wrote some four hundred years later and the story served as a lesson in old Roman plainness; the age of Cincinnatus in 458 is not given (the game makes him a man in his late fifties); the armour and the Aequi's equipment follow the period in general, not a source for this battle.
