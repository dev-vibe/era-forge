# What the Cincinnatus slice needed that the engine did not offer (job fw-F6, 29 Oct 2026)

The list for the framework. The slice (`games/cincinnatus/`) was built with **no engine edit**: Ronin's build output stayed byte-identical (`game_code.js`, `game_assets.js`, the root pages and every `pub_*` page compared with `main`). Every gap below was worked around inside the game folder, and each workaround is marked where it lives. The engine files the slice uses are listed in `tools/engine_files.json` (13 core, 6 systems, 3 + 3 + 1 kit files).

## Job fw-F5 (29 Oct 2026): the framework is its own package

The slice moved from the Ronin repo's `games/cincinnatus/` to the framework's `sample-game/`; paths below that say `games/cincinnatus/`, `src/` or `tools/engine_files.json` are the Ronin repo's of that day (the engine's files are now `core/`, `systems/` and `kits/`). G18: the slice now builds on the framework's plain theme, as ink-washi stays with Ronin; a Roman theme is still a game job. G17 and G20 stay open.

## Status after job fw-F5a (29 Oct 2026): G1-G16, G19 and G21 are CLOSED; G17, G18 and G20 stay open

Stage 1 `5477d9c` took Ronin out of the engine, stage 2 `02aee9c` made the tools and tests per game, stage 3 (commit `66f3977`) put the slice back onto the framework and deleted every workaround: `00_engine_names.js`, `10_fallback_art.js`, the `content` shims in `20_content.js`, its own lane in `40_stakewall.js`, the party filtering in `30_story.js` and `games/cincinnatus/tools/`. The proof is `tests/zero_names_test.py` (the built Cincinnatus page, comments included, names no Ronin person, place or thing), `tests/cincinnatus_test.py` (the whole slice plays on its own art, and the engine's stand-ins fill in for art that is stripped), and Ronin's own screens and battle effects, which stay pixel-identical to `main`.

## Painterly art integration, 29 October 2026

The hero-pose part of G2 is fixed: `memberCastId` now reads `GAME.hero.cast`, preserving Ronin's existing Hayato fallback. Titus's delivered pose sheet appears in battle. Other G2 text/speaker-name leftovers remain.

G13 is partly improved: animation categories can declare a validated repository-local `assetRoot`; Cincinnatus's 25 atlas playback clips now use its own game directory. Other tool paths remain as listed above.

**G20, painterly fit/rendering:** the walking slicer needed an opt-in uniform `fit` cap to preserve spear tips inside 84 x 100 cells. The existing pipeline still quantizes sprites, hardens alpha and the engine scales with nearest-neighbour; at game size this loses brush texture and visibly steps limbs/weapon edges. No broad magenta halos were seen, but isolated bright/pink weapon pixels remain. A future style-aware pipeline/rendering option should be evaluated by F4/F5; no new filtering or painted correction was introduced here. Attempts and mask failures: `art-handoff-phase2.md`.

**G21, no ground-mask tracer for a game:** `tools/scene_tool.py mask` is configured per Ronin scene (`tools/scene_tool.json`). The slice added `games/cincinnatus/tools/trace_ground.py` (threshold, opening, the region connected to `start`, holes as `nowalk`); it belongs in the scene command with a `--game` argument.
